# Public Sector & NGO Directory – IE, UK, NI

A curated, open-source directory of government departments, agencies, and major non-governmental organisations in Ireland, the United Kingdom, and Northern Ireland.  
All data is provided in ready‑to‑use Markdown tables with official websites and public enquiry email addresses.

## Features

- **Three comprehensive directories** – Ireland (IE), United Kingdom (UK), and Northern Ireland (NI).
- **Categorised entries** – each organisation is labelled as a Government Department, State Body/Agency, or NGO.
- **Markdown‑first format** – no databases to set up; simply copy the table you need.
- **Hyperlinked websites** – every entry includes a clickable link to the official site.
- **Contact emails** – official public enquiry addresses where available; alternative contact notes are given otherwise.
- **Ready for GitHub** – tables are clean, properly aligned, and ready to drop into your own documentation or README.
- **Open data** – free for anyone to use, fork, or contribute to.

## Directory contents

### Ireland (IE)
- 18 government departments
- 80+ state bodies and agencies (Teagasc, HSE, Gardaí, Enterprise Ireland, EPA, etc.)
- 40+ major NGOs (Amnesty, Focus Ireland, Irish Cancer Society, Simon Communities, etc.)

### United Kingdom (UK)
- 20 UK government departments and non‑ministerial departments
- 70+ executive agencies, regulators, and public bodies (HMRC, NHS England, Ofcom, Met Office, FCA, etc.)
- 40+ UK‑wide NGOs (Shelter, Mind, Age UK, Oxfam, RSPCA, etc.)

### Northern Ireland (NI)
- 9 NI Executive departments
- 40+ NI‑specific agencies and public bodies (Invest NI, NIHE, PSNI, Translink, etc.)
- 20+ Northern Ireland NGOs (NICVA, Women’s Aid NI, Extern, Ulster Wildlife, etc.)
